## Can xem lai tren drone THAT (khong sua trong SITL, khong ghi xuong Pixhawk)

- Cong tac mode (kenh 6) co ['STABILIZE', 'STABILIZE', 'STABILIZE', 'POSHOLD', 'STABILIZE', 'LAND'] - KHONG CO GUIDED. Tren drone that pilot khong gat duoc sang GUIDED, cu chi se khong dieu khien duoc.
- Cong tac mode KHONG CO LOITER. STOP cua he cu chi tra ve LOITER; can mot vi tri LOITER (hoac POSHOLD) de pilot lay lai quyen.
- FS_THR_ENABLE = 0: MAT TIN HIEU TAY CAM KHONG CO FAILSAFE. Drone 12 kg nen bat (vd 1 = RTL / 3 = LAND).
- BATT_FS_LOW_ACT = BATT_FS_CRT_ACT = 0: failsafe pin TAT.
- BATT_LOW_VOLT = 10.5 V la nguong cua pin 3S; pin 12S can ~42 V.
- BATT_CAPACITY = 3300 mAh qua nho cho drone 12 kg 12S - kiem lai dung luong pin that.
- COMPASS_USE = 0 tren ca 3 la ban, trong khi EK3_SRC1_YAW = 1 (la ban). DA DO TREN SITL 2026-09-15: bo tham so nay bao 'Need Position Estimate', KHONG arm duoc o GUIDED tren mat dat -> cu chi TAKEOFF se khong bao gio chay. Bat COMPASS_USE (sau khi hieu chinh la ban) thi arm + takeoff binh thuong. SITL tam dung --compass-on.
- FENCE_ENABLE = 0: khong co hang rao dia ly.
- LOIT_SPEED_MS = 12.5 m/s (mac dinh) - nhanh cho giai doan thu cu chi.
- RC5_OPTION = 31 (Motor Emergency Stop tren kenh 5) - DUNG nhu thiet ke an toan, giu nguyen.
